OUR STORY / EST. 2020

A SMALLER, SLOWER WAY

Make room
for the good.

We fell for matcha in the way you fall for a good ritual: one cup at a time. The color, the craft, the gentle lift. We wanted to share that feeling without making it complicated.

So we work with growers who care for the leaf long before it reaches your bowl, and keep our blends honest, expressive, and easy to enjoy.
