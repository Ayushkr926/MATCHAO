A GOOD CUP, MADE SIMPLE

Whisk up
some calm.

Just a bowl, a whisk, and a few minutes. Here's the simple method we use for a smooth, bright cup at home.

THE MATCHAO BREW GUIDE
A moment for yourself · 3 minutes

FIRST, PICK YOUR CUP

One little ritual.
Your way.

Choose a traditional bowl for a softer, concentrated sip, or make it over ice for a brighter daily latte. The ingredient amounts update for the number of servings.

Servings1
Matchathe good green stuff2 g
Waterwarm, not boiling60 ml
Milkoat or your favorite180 ml
01

Warm your bowl

Pour in a little warm water, swirl it around, then tip it out. A warm bowl helps the matcha bloom evenly.

02

Sift, then add water

Sift the matcha into your bowl to keep it silky. Add a splash of warm water, around 80°C / 175°F.

03

Whisk with a light hand

Move your whisk quickly in a loose zigzag until a fine layer of foam appears. No need to press against the bowl.

04

Make it your moment

Sip as is, or pour over ice and top with milk for a creamy matcha latte. Take the first sip slowly.

Now you just need the good green stuff.
